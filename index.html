Frequently Asked Questions

What is the statutory timeframe for building plan approvals?

Under the Municipal Citizen's Charter, all standard residential and commercial building plans must be scrutinized and disposed of within 30 working days from the date of complete document submission and fee verification.

Who can submit a building plan application?

Applications must be submitted by the legal property owner or through a Council of Architecture (CoA) registered architect or licensed municipal building surveyor.

What happens if a department raises a deficiency?

The applicant receives an SMS and portal alert. The statutory 30-day clock pauses. The applicant has 15 days to resubmit the corrected drawing or required document directly via the application details page.
